Lumo brings your whole neighborhood into one app. Pick how you want to use it and get an experience built just for that.

ORDER AS A CUSTOMER

• Instant alerts for every new order

FIND OR LIST A HOME
• Browse homes for sale, long-term rentals and short stays
• List sales and long-term rentals free
• Short-term rentals pay just 5% per booking

No subscriptions. No hidden fees. Available in 15+ languages.
