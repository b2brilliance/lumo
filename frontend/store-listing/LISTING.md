## Full description (both stores, 4000 max)
Lumo brings your whole neighborhood into one app. Pick how you want to use it and get an experience built just for that.

ORDER AS A CUSTOMER
• Order from restaurants, food trucks and local shops for pickup or delivery
• Book local pros for cleaning, repairs, moving, yard work and more
• See the delivery fee up front — based on distance and order size

EARN AS A WORKER
• Pick up delivery runs and service jobs near you
• Set your own hours and keep 90% of every job
• Get paid the same day

SELL AS A BUSINESS OR INDIVIDUAL
• Free shop for restaurants, food trucks, stores and personal sellers
• Add your menu or products with photos, prices and stock
• Simple 5% service fee only when you make a sale

FIND OR LIST A HOME
• Browse homes for sale, long-term rentals and short stays
• List sales and long-term rentals free
• Short-term rentals pay just 5% per booking

No subscriptions. No hidden fees. Available in 15+ languages.
